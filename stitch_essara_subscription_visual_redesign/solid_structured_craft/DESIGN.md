## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-clarity, confident, and tactile interface tailored for audit-level financial visibility without the clinical coldness of traditional fintech. It bridges structural discipline and playful approachability—merging the deliberate geometry of architectural print media with modern web craftsmanship.

### Brand Personality & Core Audience
- **Target Audience:** Modern digital natives, freelancers, founders, and households managing sprawling SaaS, streaming, cloud, and subscription stacks who feel fatigued by subtle, low-contrast, evasive micro-billing.
- **Personality Attributes:** Vigilant yet reassuring, tactile, razor-sharp, transparent, and unapologetically definitive. 
- **Emotional Intent:** Transform the anxiety of "money leaking silently in the background" into the satisfying, empowering tactile sensation of snapping physical puzzle pieces into place and physically cutting out waste.

### Aesthetic Movement
The design movement is **Neo-Structured Craft / Friendly Neo-Brutalist Fintech**:
- **Tactile Hard Offsets:** Deep, un-blurred solid shadow planes establish an unambiguous spatial hierarchy. Elements feel stamped, printed, or precision-cut from heavy card stock.
- **Crisp Structural Outlines:** Every container declares its exact boundaries through deliberate 1.5px to 2px borders, rejecting ambiguous gradients or fuzzy edges.
- **Vibrant Functional Semantics:** Bold indigo anchors navigation and critical mechanics, tempered by an organic warm-cream backdrop, punchy leak-detection alerts, and vibrant resolution emeralds.

## Colors

The palette avoids sterile grays in favor of an artisanal warm base grounded by deep ink accents and hyper-expressive functional colors.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#4338CA` - Deep Cobalt):** Represents structural authority, primary actions, and system mechanics. Used for active navigation tabs, major CTA buttons, key metrics, and primary toggle states.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Emerald Shield):** Denotes optimized expenditure, resolved redundancies, safe billing statuses, and confirmed monetary savings.
- **Tertiary (`#F43F5E` - Leak Crimson):** Dedicated to critical alerts, overlapping active subscriptions, dormant seat leaks, and destructive actions.
- **Warning Accent (`#F59E0B` - Amber Flare):** Applied to renewal alerts within 48 hours, upcoming price hikes, and unreviewed card authorizations.
- **Neutral Primary (`#181826` - Deep Ink Slate):** Used for typography, hard structural borders, and the zero-blur solid offset drop shadows.
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F6F2` - Warm Alabaster):** A tactile, newsprint-tinted paper background that diminishes screen glare and provides an organic foundation.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Clean container fill providing immediate contrast against the alabaster canvas.

### Dark Mode Principles
When rendering the dark theme, the canvas transitions to `#12131C` (Midnight Slate) with surface cards in `#1C1D2A`. Border lines flip to `#2E3044` with offset drop shadows cast in pitch obsidian (`#090A0F`). Primary and functional accents retain their calibrated hex identities to ensure accessibility and rapid pattern recognition across light and dark states.

## Typography

Typography pairs technical precision with organic legibility.

- **Headline & Label Voice (Space Grotesk):** Provides structured geometric character for high-impact metric counters, section headers, badges, and monetary amounts. All numerical indicators must enable `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure stable vertical alignment in financial tables, audit rows, and recurring currency columns.
- **Reading Voice (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Brings soft, balanced humanist terminals that keep dense subscription descriptions, terms, and notification cards fatigue-free and instantly scannable.
- **Case Rules:** Uppercase styling is strictly reserved for micro-badges (`label-sm`), tabular metadata categories, and status chips to maintain systematic visual discipline.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt structural rhythm within a responsive 12-column grid.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid capped at a maximum width of 1280px with centered gutters of `1.5rem` and outer margins of `2.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout utilizing `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout utilizing `0.75rem` gutters with compact `1rem` outer canvas padding to maximize screen utility.

### Flow Architecture
- **Metric Cards & Stack Overlaps:** Grouped in 3- or 4-column spans on desktop, collapsing to 2-column or stacked full-width units on mobile devices.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** A strict element stacking rule dictates that components never blend together: spacing between cards relies on explicit margins rather than dynamic floating padding. 
- **Density:** Financial feeds favor a high-density, compact vertical stack (`space-sm` between rows, `space-md` internal cell padding) to minimize scrolling friction when auditing dozens of active subscriptions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects fuzzy, diffused, or ambient blur shadows entirely. Depth is achieved strictly through **Hard-Edged Solid Offset Drop Shadows** combined with crisp borders, establishing a tactile, physical card layer.

### The Depth Scale
- **Flat Ground (Level 0):** Canvas surfaces, dividers, and background data channels. Borders are 1.5px solid `#181826` without any shadow offset.
- **Static Containers / Cards (Level 1):** Offset shadow of `4px 4px 0px #181826` with a `1.5px` border. Applied to overview modules, telemetry tiles, and financial cards.
- **Interactive Surfaces (Level 2):** Offset shadow of `6px 6px 0px #181826` with a `2px` border. Used for default-state call-to-action buttons, key metric feature tiles, and prominent filter bars.
- **Overlays / Drawers / Modals (Level 3):** Offset shadow of `8px 8px 0px #181826` with a `2px` border. Creates unequivocal foreground distinction for subscription cancellation flows and deep-dive audits.

### Interactive Physics
Interactive elements simulate physical tactile buttons:
- **Hover State:** The element translates `2px` upward and leftward (`translate(-2px, -2px)`), expanding the solid offset shadow from `4px` to `6px`.
- **Active / Pressed State:** The element collapses downward and rightward (`translate(4px, 4px)`), reducing the offset shadow to `0px 0px 0px #181826`. This delivers instant, tactile feedback that the action has been physically executed.

## Shapes

The interface balances hard geometric stability with friendly, ergonomic corners (`0.5rem` standard radius). This prevents the visual system from drifting into harsh, uninviting industrial brutalism.

### Geometric Syntax
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** `roundedness: 2` (8px standard corner radius). Creates soft corners that comfortably hold tight 1.5px and 2px borders.
- **Containers & Big Stat Modules:** Use `1rem` (16px) radius to maintain internal-to-external border curve proportions.
- **Pill Badges & Status Chips:** Utilize a full circular radius (`9999px`) to create an instant optical contrast against rectangular financial tiles and stat cards.
- **Geometric Accents:** Subtle background accents utilize 45-degree corner chamfers on tooltips, pure circular category indicators, and an optional 12px structural dot-grid pattern (`radial-gradient(#181826 1px, transparent 1px)`) set at 8% opacity in empty states.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Cobalt fill (`#4338CA`), crisp white typography (`Space Grotesk`, semi-bold), 2px solid `#181826` border, and a `4px 4px 0px #181826` solid offset shadow.
- **Destructive / Leak Action:** Leak Crimson fill (`#F43F5E`), crisp white text, 2px solid `#181826` border, same offset shadow physics.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Warm Alabaster (`#F7F6F2`) or White fill, `#181826` text, 1.5px border, and `2px 2px 0px #181826` offset.
- **Interaction:** Hover triggers `-2px` translation with an enlarged shadow; active click reduces the shadow to `0px` with a positive translation.

### Cards & Stat Tiles
- Background in `#FFFFFF` with a 2px `#181826` outline and `4px 4px 0px #181826` shadow.
- Stat tiles feature an isolated top header section separated by a 1.5px horizontal border rule, housing the metric category and an optional pill badge indicator.
- Numbers utilize `tabular-stat` styling in bold Space Grotesk.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- High-contrast pills (`roundedness: 9999px`) with a 1.5px `#181826` border.
- **Critical Leak Chip:** `#F43F5E` background with white text or a light crimson tint (`#FFE4E6`) with `#9F1239` text and a solid outline.
- **Savings Chip:** `#10B981` background with white text, or light emerald tint with `#065F46` text.
- Chips never display drop shadows to ensure clear visual hierarchy underneath parent cards.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background in `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border in `#181826`.
- Typography in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with `#6B7280` placeholder color.
- **Focus State:** 2px border in `#4338CA` accompanied by a distinct `3px 3px 0px #4338CA` solid shadow offset. No glowing blur rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square with a 4px corner radius for checkboxes; pure circles for radios.
- 2px solid `#181826` border.
- **Checked State:** Fill turns to primary Cobalt (`#4338CA`) with a stark white geometric checkmark and a `2px 2px 0px #181826` drop shadow.

### Overlap & Redundancy Alert Banner (Domain Specific)
- Dual-toned warning component: 2px solid `#181826` frame with a `6px 6px 0px #F43F5E` crimson-tinted solid drop shadow.
- Displays side-by-side logos of overlapping subscriptions (e.g., streaming or cloud storage) connected by an amber/crimson dotted bridge, with immediate "Consolidate & Save" action triggers.
